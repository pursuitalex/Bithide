No own wallet

When your business doesn’t have its own wallet

  • Third party providers handle your customers’ deposits and withdrawals.
  • External providers charge fees on every transaction.
  • Customers switch between services to complete transactions.
  • You miss touchpoints to communicate with your customers.
  • Your product depends on third party terms, fees and uptime.

When the wallet becomes your infrastructure

  • More customer financial activity stays inside your business.
  • Your business may earn from transaction fees and markups.
  • Wallet activity becomes valuable customer intelligence.
  • Push, referral and affiliate tools support retention and acquisition.
  • Your business controls the infrastructure, fees, data and operating rules.
